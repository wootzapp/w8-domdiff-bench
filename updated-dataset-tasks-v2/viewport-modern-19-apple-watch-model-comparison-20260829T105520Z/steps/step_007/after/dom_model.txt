- Eligibility
- Those eligible to purchase from the Apple Store and receive Education Pricing include faculty, staff, students and parents as follows:
- K-12 - Any employee of a public or private K-12 institution in the United States or Puerto Rico is eligible, including homeschool teachers. In addition, school board members who are currently serving as elected or appointed members are eligible. PTA or PTO executives currently serving as elected or appointed officers are eligible.
