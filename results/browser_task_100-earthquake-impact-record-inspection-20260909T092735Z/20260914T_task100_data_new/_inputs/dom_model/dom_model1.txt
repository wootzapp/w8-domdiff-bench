- Tectonic Summary
- The March 11, 2011, M 9.1 Tohoku earthquake, which occurred near the northeast coast of Honshu, Japan, resulted from shallow thrust faulting on the subduction zone plate boundary between the Pacific and North America plates. At the location of this earthquake, the Pacific plate moves roughly westward relative to the North America plate at a velocity of 83 mm/yr, and begins its westward descent beneath Japan at the Japan Trench, east of the March 11th earthquake.
