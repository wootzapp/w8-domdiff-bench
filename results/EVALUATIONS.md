# Browser evidence evaluations

The benchmark's methods and evaluation results are documented in
[docs/technical-report.md](../docs/technical-report.md). It includes all
aggregate tables, sensitivity analyses, model provenance, website blockers,
execution failures, and the criterion-level findings generated by
`gpt-5.6-sol` inventory and `gemini-3.1-pro-preview` adjudication. It distinguishes automated judgments, report category annotations and internal review roles.

The report also contains the mathematical definitions, CLI success rules,
research references, action-generation model, and representation sizes.

Selected results are in the [repository README](../README.md#evaluation-results).
The [results index](README.md) describes the machine-readable
[summary](summary.json).

The matched comparison uses **97 tasks and 619 criteria per run**. Successful, failed and errored recordings with usable evidence are included. Recordings with no captured state remain unscored, not zero-loss audits.
