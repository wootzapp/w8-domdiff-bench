# Browser evidence evaluations

The [technical report](../docs/technical-report.md) describes the methods,
models and criterion-level findings. The evaluation covers 92 tasks; completed
evidence audits cover 91 tasks and 582 criteria.

Of 100 recorded tasks, 92 had usable, aligned evidence for evaluation; 8 were excluded because captures were missing/incomplete or failed URL alignment. Evidence-loss results cover 91 tasks because task 76’s audit failed validation.

The [results index](README.md) and [summary](summary.json) provide the counts
and exclusions. The [Hugging Face audits](https://huggingface.co/datasets/WootzappLab/browser-agent-tasks/tree/a10226a46d3a9c3ee5f0f40b244163d2a6b50e75/evaluation)
retain the complete source findings and citations.
